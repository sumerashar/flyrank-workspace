01 / THE ROUTINE

How I'll add my next case study

Every case study will follow the same three-beat structure: problem, what I did, and what came of it.

STEP 01

The problem

Explain the real problem, who it affects, and why it matters. Keep the context specific and easy to understand.

STEP 02

What I did

Describe my contribution, tools, technical decisions, implementation, and supporting links or screenshots.

STEP 03

What came of it

Report measured results and lessons learned. If impact has not been measured, I will say so honestly.

My publishing checklist

Select a completed project → gather evidence → write the three sections → verify claims → publish → test the link.
